The Internet technology has helped desgin a large number of web sites to
facilitate social relations among people around the world. These are known as
social networking services or social networks or social media. At present, Face-
book is the most popular social media site. LinkedIn, X (formerly Twitter), Google+ etc.
are other frequently used social services. Social network services are web-based
and hence, provide ways for the users to interact through the Internet. These
services make it possible to connect people across the borders and thus have
made the users feel that they really live in a global village.
