July Declaration

The July Declaration serves as a mechanism to officially bind future governments to
the principles and demands of the July Uprising, which represents the citizens' utmost
desires. On August 5, 2025, the first anniversary of the uprising, the Chief Advisor of
the Interim Government, Dr. Muhammad Yunus, read out the declaration. The
document expresses the intent to provide legal protection to the ‘National Heroes’ and
those who were injured or killed. Prepared in consultation with political parties, the
declaration further states that the government will grant constitutional and state
recognition to the student-led mass uprising.
