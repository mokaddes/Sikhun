E. The Butterfly Effect
The butterfly effect is a concept that suggests small, seemingly insignificant
actions or events can have large, unpredictable consequences over time. The name
comes from the idea that the flap of a butterfly’s wings in one part of the world, for
example, Bangladesh, could cause a tiny change in air pressure that eventually
causes a tornado in Peru. This illustrates how minor actions in life can set off a
chain of events resulting in major outcomes.
