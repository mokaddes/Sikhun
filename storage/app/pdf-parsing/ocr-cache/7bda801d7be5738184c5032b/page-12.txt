ছা. Read the texts below defining three types of literary work and decide what
type of work “The Parrot’s Tale” is.

Satire: a genre of literature (and sometimes graphic and performing arts) in
which vices, follies, abuses, and shortcomings are held up to ridicule, ideally
with the intent of shaming individuals, corporations, government, or society
itself into improvement.

Tragedy: a play dealing with tragic events and having an unhappy ending,
especially one concerning the downfall of the main character.

Comedy: a kind of literary work that is amusing and satirical or humorous in
tone. Generally it has a happy ending.
