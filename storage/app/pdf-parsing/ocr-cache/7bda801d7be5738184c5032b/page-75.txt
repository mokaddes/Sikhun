This then is what the ANC is fighting. Their struggle is a truly national one. It is a

struggle of the African people, inspired by their own suffering and their own

experience. It is a struggle for the right to live.

During my lifetime I have dedicated myself to this struggle of the African people.

Ihave fought against white domination, and I have fought against black

domination. I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society in which all

persons live together in harmony and with equal opportunities. It is an ideal which

I hope to live for and to achieve. But if needs be, it is an ideal for which I am

prepared to die.

Nelson Mandela - April 20, 1964 **
