« Management of chronic health conditions

Yoga might help in a variety of health conditions, such as cancer,
depression, pain, anxiety and insomnia, fatigue and mood shifts. Yoga
can also help reduce heart rate and blood pressure.

* Weight loss

If you're overweight or have eating disorder, yoga may help you make
the healthy lifestyle changes necessary to gain control of your eating and
reduce weight.

While you shouldn't expect yoga to cure you or offer you 100 percent relief, it
can help some health conditions when combined with standard medical
treatment. And if you already enjoy good health, yoga can be an enjoyable
supplement to your regular fitness routine.
