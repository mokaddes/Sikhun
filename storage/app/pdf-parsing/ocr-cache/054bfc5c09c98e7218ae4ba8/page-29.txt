Tiya : Anusha, what's the matter? You’re wearing sports trousers, T-shirts,
sports shoes and carrying a bag! Where are you coming from?
And you look tired!

Anusha _ : Not exactly, Tiya. I’m feeling great because I’m just coming back

Tiya : Do you go to the gym regularly?

Anusha : Yes, I do. I go twice a week. It’s one of my favourite pastimes.

Tiya : Really! Going to the gym is your favourite pastime! You make
me laugh!

Anusha : Why not? I like sports because I like to be fit. I’m not a lazy
person like you!

Tiya : What do you do there?

Anusha : Ido yoga.

Tiya : Don’t you have any other pastimes?

Anusha : Hmm... yes, Ihave. I like playing chess, painting, and reading

books when I’m free. I also like photography but I’m yet to learn
