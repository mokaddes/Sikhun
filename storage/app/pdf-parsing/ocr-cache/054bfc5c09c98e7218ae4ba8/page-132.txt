A. Shanta was chatting with one of her foreign friends Jenny. Read

the dialogue and tell what the subject of their conversation is.

Jenny; Shanta, what’s your dream for the future?

Shanta; To bea good human being first and then work for my country-no
matter whether I become a social worker, an artist, or a professional.

Jenny : Wow! I really appreciate your thought! By the way, Shanta, does

anyone inspire you in your dreams?

Shanta: Certainly. I'm always influenced by the great personalities in our coun-
try and abroad. Whenever | read the biography of great people, I try to
understand how their dreams helped them to be what they have
been.

Jenny : Would you please tell me about some of your favourite dreamers?

Shanta : Sure! Read the following texts to know about some dreamers

in my country. They are my icons as well.
