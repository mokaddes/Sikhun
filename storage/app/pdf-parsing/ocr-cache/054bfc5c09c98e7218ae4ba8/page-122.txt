B. Read the text and answer the questions that follow.

What type of job do you want to have? Is it a highly paid and high-profile
conventional job most people like? Yes, you can have it. It is matter of your
choice and an important decision that would help you advance in your life. But
have you ever thought of other types of jobs out there--- cool, fun, exciting, even
strange or weird jobs you never knew existed? What are they? Did you ever
wonder who picks up the chewing gum left on a park bench or who assesses
whether a piece of furniture is comfortable enough or who constructs glass eye-
balls or who farms oysters in the middle of the ocean? These are all odd,
unexpected, wild, unconventional, strange or just plain weird jobs and careers.
They are not your traditional career choices, but somebody out there does all
these things, gets paid for it, and enjoys life like anybody else.
