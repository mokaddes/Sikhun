Bhutanese houses are built from mud and stone, with wooden shingle roof. The
Bhutanese never use iron nails in their buildings. Usually, the Bhutanese build
each other's houses by exchanging labour within the community. Different
Festivals are celebrated all year round in Bhutan. Colourful masks are used in the
festivals which reflect the rich Bhutanese culture. Dances are performed by the
Buddhist monks to protect the valleys and ward off evil spirits. The main
religious festival is called Tshechus. The teachings of Lord Buddha are enacted
through mask dances for three to five days in the courtyard of the monasteries.
People attend these events in their best clothes, carrying picnic baskets. Another
major festival is called Losar. It is celebrated on Lunar New Year. People cook
special dishes and wear new clothes. It is a time for family get-together. Men
play archery or darts while women sing and dance.
