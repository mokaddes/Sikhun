Nishat Mazumder, a Bangladeshi woman in her early forties, is a sports icon. She
comes of a modest background; her father is a businessman and her mother is a
housewife. She is the second of four siblings. Nishat has three things that she
can be proud of. These are: she has extraordinarily supportive parents who
accepted her dreams as real; her father was a freedom fighter in the Liberation
War of Bangladesh in 1971, and she scaled the highest peak in the world as
the first Bangladeshi girl in 2012. Nishat’s life presents an inspirational story
that the youth in Bangladesh needs to know,
