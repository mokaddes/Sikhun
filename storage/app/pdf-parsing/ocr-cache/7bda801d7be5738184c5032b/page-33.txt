Scenario 1:

A group of college students were watching television news in their hostel. They
were touched by the plight of the flood affected people in Sunamgonj. All the
rivers and water bodies there had overflowed their banks because of the onrush of
flood water from the hills beyond our border. Their homes, hats, bazars, shops,
and crop lands were under several feet of water. The local administration had
rescued the people and accommodated them in schools that were on higher
grounds. Villagers in the nearby areas were helping these people in all possible
ways. Yet, ensuring drinking water, cleanliness and hygiene was a big challenge.
The schools had toilets but these were clearly not enough. Seeing this situation,
Ahmed, Swapan, Rakib, and Niladri quickly decided to go to Sunamgonj and
work for the flood victims. But to do that they needed money which they did not

have.
