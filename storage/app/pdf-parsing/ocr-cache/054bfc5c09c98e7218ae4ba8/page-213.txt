10. A Lesson in Priorities

Rana was running late for work, as usual. His morning routine was a busy
one—skipping breakfast, barely managing to catch the bus. That morning,
as he rushed out of his house, his phone rang. It was his mother. He
declined the call, thinking to call her back later. On the bus, he felt a pang
of guilt but brushed it off, focusing on his workday ahead. At the office, he
was swamped with meetings and deadlines. By evening, he finally
checked his phone and saw multiple missed calls from his mother. He got
worried and he called her back. His mother’s voice trembled as she told
him his father had been hospitalized. Rana’s heart sank. He realized he had
been too busy to prioritize his family. He rushed to the hospital, where his
father was recovering. From that day on, Rana promised to balance work
and family, understanding that some things are more important than a busy
schedule.
