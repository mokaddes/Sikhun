tomorrow, I still have a dream. It is a dream deeply rooted in the American dream,

Thave a dream that one day this nation will rise up and live out the true meaning of

Thave a dream that one day on the red hills of Georgia, the sons of former slaves
and the sons of former slave owners will be able to sit down together at the table of
brotherhood.

Ihave a dream that my four little children will one day live in a nation where they
