Antonio was a good and kind man. He always helped the poor. The people of
Venice loved him very much for his honesty and kindness.

Antonio had a close friend named Bassanio. He was a handsome young man and
was born ina noble family. Bassanio liked to live a very luxurious life. He loved
grandeur and style. He spent more money than he earned. As a result, he was
very often short of money. In such situations, Bassanio would go to his best
friend Antonio for help. Antonio would help him with cash.

It so happened that Bassanio fell in love with a wealthy lady named Portia. Portia
was known not only for her beauty but also for her wisdom. She was soft
towards Bassanio too. He wanted to visit Portia in a grand manner but he did not
have any money. So he went to Antonio.

Bassanio said, "Dear friend Antonio, I am in great need of some money. I would
like to visit Portia at Belmont, grandly dressed and with many servants. But I
don't have any money right now. Please help me to fulfill my intention."
