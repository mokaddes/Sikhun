“Life is not easy for any of us. But what of that? We

Do you know who was the first woman to receive a Nobel Prize? Or the first
person in history to achieve the remarkable feat of winning two Nobel Prizes? Do
you know who is the only woman to receive two Nobel Prizes, and in two different
scientific fields?

The answer to all of these questions points to one extraordinary individual: Maria
Salomea Sktodowska-Curie, more famously known as Marie Curie.
