Now, complete the story about a village girl called Urmila, who became
champion in a national swimming competition at the age of 14. Write no more
than 180 words.

Urmila was a little girl who grew up in a village named Patuair in Kishoreganj
district. A small river called Phuleswari flowed through her village. At the age of
five, Urmila used to go to the river with her father and started to learn swimming.
Her mother did not like the idea of her daughter swimming in the river. But her
father was happy to train Urmila to be a competent swimmer. Who knew that the
little girl would become an accomplished swimmer one day!
