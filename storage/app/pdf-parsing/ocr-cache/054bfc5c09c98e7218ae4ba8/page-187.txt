Scientists have identified Hydrogen as another form of renewable energy source.
It is the most abundant element in nature. But it does not exist separately as a
gas. It is always combined with other elements, such as with oxygen to make
water. Hydrogen, separated from another element, can be burned as a fuel to
produce electricity.

Our Earth's interior contains molten lava which gives off extreme heat. This heat
inside the Earth produces steam and hot water which can be used as
geothermal energy to produce electricity for heating homes, etc.

Ocean energy comes from several sources. Ocean's force of tide and wave can
be used to produce energy. The surface of the ocean gets more heat from the sun
than the ocean depths. This temperature difference can be used as energy source
too.
