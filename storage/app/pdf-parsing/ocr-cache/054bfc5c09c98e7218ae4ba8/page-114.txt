D. Read the text and answer the following questions.

The French sculptor Frederic Auguste Bartholdi was assigned to design and
complete a sculpture Liberty Enlightening the World within 1876, so that it
could be a gift for the Americans on the occasion of the hundredth anniversary
of the American Declaration of Independence. The statue was a joint venture
between the USA and France. The French people built the statue and
assemble it in the States, and the people of the USA built the pedestal for the
statue.

Raising money for the pedestal was completed in August 1885. The construction
of the pedestal was finished in April 1886. In the meantime, France completed
the Statue in July 1884. They sent it to New York by the French warship
‘Isere' in 1885. While transporting the statue, it was split up into 350 pieces and
packed in 214 crates.
